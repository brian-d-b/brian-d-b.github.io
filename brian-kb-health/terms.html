Purpose and availability

Brian KB Health is independent personal software for its owner to retrieve and organize their own Google Health records. It is not a public subscription service and does not offer public account registration. These pages provide information about the integration.

Google Health access depends on the owner's authorization, the availability of Google's API and the connected devices and services. Collection may be delayed, interrupted or unavailable. Software and supported features may change.

Understanding the records

Device readings, API summaries and derived analyses can be incomplete or inaccurate. Current-day totals may be provisional, and earlier records can be revised. Comparisons and AI-generated interpretations do not establish causes.

This project does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, professional medical advice or emergency monitoring. Its records and analyses are intended for personal understanding.

Authorization and privacy

The connection uses the owner's authorized read-only access. It does not grant permission to access someone else's account or change their data. Data handling, retained copies and revocation are described in the privacy policy.

The owner can stop using the integration and revoke its Google connection. Removing the connection and deleting retained records are separate actions.

Independent project

Brian KB Health is maintained by the project owner. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Fitbit. Google, Fitbit and other referenced services remain subject to their own terms and policies.

For questions, use the developer support contact shown on the Google consent screen. Do not include sensitive health or authorization information in public messages.

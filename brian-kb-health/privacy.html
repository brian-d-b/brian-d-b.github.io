Privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Brian KB Health is a personal-use integration operated by its owner. This policy describes the Google Health connection and the private records it supplies. The public information site does not expose those records.

Data accessed

With the owner's Google authorization, the integration reads data available through these permission categories:

  • Activity and fitness, including available activity totals, heart-rate samples and exercise records.
  • Sleep, including available sessions, timing, stages and summaries.
  • Health metrics and measurements, including available recovery and vital measurements.

Records may include source identifiers, timestamps, units, device or platform information and revision metadata. Authorization credentials are retained separately so scheduled collection can continue. The integration does not receive the owner's Google password. It requests read-only access and does not modify Google Health data.

Use of the data

The data supports the owner's personal recordkeeping, dashboards, summaries, comparisons and exploration of recorded patterns. Collection and validation software preserves source evidence and distinguishes missing, incomplete and revised records. The private knowledge base can combine these records with other information the owner has chosen to keep.

Authorized AI assistants may process relevant records when the owner requests analysis or has authorized a workflow that uses them. This can involve sending the necessary records to the AI service selected by the owner. The integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.

Brian KB Health's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and access

Retrieved records are stored as private source snapshots in the owner's knowledge base, including storage synchronized through services the owner uses. Derived records are stored in a private local database and may appear in private reports or assistant conversations. Credentials are stored separately with access restrictions.

The owner and software or services authorized by the owner can access the data for these purposes. The public website and its public source repository do not contain health records, private account identifiers or authorization credentials.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Source snapshots and derived records are retained for personal history and reproducible analysis until the owner chooses to remove them. There is no automatic deletion deadline.

The owner can stop scheduled collection and revoke this app's access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized reads; it does not automatically delete copies already retrieved.

Deletion is managed by the owner across source snapshots, derived databases, synchronized copies and relevant reports. Copies held in backups or authorized assistant services may require separate deletion using those services' controls. This site does not provide an automated deletion form or endpoint.

This information website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, cookies or sign-in flow. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, under its own policies. External links lead to services with their own privacy practices.

Questions and changes

The project owner's public profile identifies the maintainer. For privacy, access or deletion questions, use the developer support contact shown on the Google consent screen. Do not post health records, authorization codes or other sensitive information in public issues.

This policy will be updated if the integration's data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.

Personal software · Read-only access

Your records, organized for personal understanding.

Brian KB Health connects the owner's Google Health account to a private personal knowledge base. It retrieves permitted measurements, preserves their source and timing, and makes them available for personal summaries, comparisons and authorized assistant workflows.

What the integration reads

  • Activity and fitness: available steps, distance, energy, heart rate and exercise records.
  • Sleep: available sleep sessions, timing, stages and related summaries.
  • Health metrics and measurements: available readings such as heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, oxygen saturation, breathing rate and temperature.

Access is limited to the three read-only Google Health permission categories approved by the owner. Availability depends on the connected devices and records. This integration does not change or delete records in Google Health.

How it is used

Scheduled software collects and validates records for a private database and personal views. The owner can use authorized AI assistants to examine these records, compare periods and explore questions about recorded habits. Missing data, provisional totals and derived values remain distinguishable.

This is a personal-use project for its owner. It does not offer public account registration, medical care or emergency monitoring. These public pages describe the integration; they do not display health records or provide access to the private knowledge base.

Access and control

Google authorization happens through Google's consent flow. The owner can stop collection and revoke the connection from their Google Account. Retained copies are managed separately, as explained in the privacy policy.
